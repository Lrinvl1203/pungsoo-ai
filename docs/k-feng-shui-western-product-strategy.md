# K-Pungsoo Western Product Strategy

## 1. Positioning

Use **Pungsoo (풍수)** as the owned Korean category name and explain it once as
“Korean spatial balance.” “K-Feng Shui” can be an acquisition keyword, but the
product itself should be presented as:

> Personalized Korean guardian art, generated from your space, site and birth elements.

Do not promise luck, wealth, health outcomes or supernatural efficacy. Sell a
personal ritual, a meaningful design story and a specific placement recommendation.

## 2. Two product ladders

### Interior Balance

1. `internal_report` — Interior Balance Reading
2. `internal_remedy` — Room Guardian Artwork
3. `internal_frame` — Room Guardian Frame
4. `internal_object` — Desk Guardian Totem

The analysis source is a room photo, room purpose and the resident’s birth
elements. The physical destinations are a wall, desk, bedside table or shelf.

### Site Guardian

1. `external_report` — Site Guardian Reading
2. `external_remedy` — Site Guardian Artwork
3. `external_frame` — Site Guardian Frame
4. `external_object` — Gate Guardian Totem

The analysis source is an address, surrounding land/water/road flow and the
owner’s birth elements. The physical destinations are an entrance, lobby,
reception desk or retail counter.

## 4. Recommended MVP

Launch only these four first:

1. Digital reading and printable guardian art
2. Framed Modern Minhwa guardian print
3. Small Personal Guardian Totem
4. Entry Guardian Plaque

Add the Maedeup charm as a low-priced checkout add-on after the core objects have
acceptable production samples.

The first 3D object should have:

- one continuous grounded base;
- no thin horns, tails, ears or detached limbs;
- three sizes at most;
- matte stone, warm brass and ink-black finishes;
- a removable name/element plate instead of regenerating the whole mesh;
- a QR card linking to the reading, not electronics embedded in the object.

## 5. Cultural design rules

- Build from documented Korean motifs: minhwa tiger and magpie, Korean zodiac
  guardians, Haechi, sotdae, dancheong color rhythm, hanji and maedeup.
- A tiger can be a premium fixed guardian because Korean tiger paintings were
  historically used to ward off harmful spirits.
- Haechi belongs to the gatekeeper/business line, not the zodiac selector.
- The Four Direction animals are shared across East Asia. If used, identify the
  product as Korean-inspired through material, palette and cited Korean references
  instead of claiming the symbols are exclusively Korean.
- Avoid bagua mirrors, Chinese coins, generic mandalas, Buddha decor and mixed
  “Asian mystic” imagery. They weaken the K-Pungsoo identity.

## 6. Go-to-market message

Lead with the object, then reveal the reading:

> Meet the guardian designed for your room.

Secondary copy:

> Its animal, color, material and placement are generated from your space,
> address and five-element profile, then reinterpreted through Korean guardian art.

Good gifting occasions:

- housewarming;
- new apartment;
- new business or studio opening;
- birthday;
- wedding or partnership;
- desk refresh / home-office gift.

## 7. Why this direction is commercially plausible

- Korea’s Ministry of Culture reported continued diversification of global Hallyu
  interest beyond music and video, with Europe and North America representing
  meaningful shares of 2025 overseas coverage.
- Korea’s official MU:DS museum merchandise grew from KRW 6.59B in 2021 to
  KRW 41.33B in 2025. A magpie-tiger badge alone reportedly sold about 90,000
  units in a year, supporting the appeal of compact, modernized Korean guardian
  motifs.
- Etsy’s 2025 trend guidance emphasizes personal, sentimental items with a story,
  expressive home decor and collectible or unexpected forms. Its Marketplace
  Insights product also exposes real-time search demand and competition, which
  should be used before finalizing English listing names.
