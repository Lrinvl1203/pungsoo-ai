# Neo K-Mystic Dragon Object — 3 Concepts

## Direction

The object language is **Neo K-Mystic / Seoul Guardian**: contemporary Seoul
fashion, art-toy, and gallery sculpture with Korean guardian energy encoded in
silhouette and negative space. It deliberately avoids literal traditional
ornament, celadon, palace decoration, and souvenir styling.

Korean cues are expressed indirectly:

- a taegeuk-like balance created by body and tail negative space;
- compressed guardian-mask proportions;
- dancheong-like rhythm reduced to sparse angular cuts;
- ink-black material and restrained point colors;
- dokkaebi-fire energy abstracted into modern incisions.

## Concepts

1. `01-midnight-dragon.png` — matte obsidian, electric-cobalt incision, upright
   S-curve. Best overall direction for the first premium product.
2. `02-red-signal-dragon.png` — gunmetal, vermilion signal line, crouching
   street-luxury guardian. Strongest character, but currently risks reading as
   mecha and needs simplification before CAD.
3. `03-moon-ink-dragon.png` — ink-black monolith, smoky inset, restrained teal
   incision. Most gallery-like and easiest to position as interior sculpture.

## Physical Prototype Target

- Finished height: 80–90 mm
- One-piece printable body with an integrated flat base
- Minimum wall/feature thickness: 2 mm after CAD conversion
- No separate whiskers, floating parts, or needle-like horns
- First prototype material: matte black resin
- Accent: one painted recessed line, not full multicolor painting

These images are design concepts, not production meshes. Before ordering, the
selected design must be rebuilt as a watertight STL/3MF, checked for balance,
minimum thickness, supports, drain holes if hollow, and post-processing access.

## Recommendation

Develop concept 1 first, borrowing the calmer gallery restraint of concept 3.
Shorten and thicken the horns, widen the base contact, and keep the blue accent
as a shallow paintable groove rather than a separate insert.
