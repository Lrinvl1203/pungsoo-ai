# Gate Guardian — Native Animal Form, 3 Samples

## Revised direction

The entrance product keeps the customer's guardian animal in its native,
recognizable form. It does **not** use the myeongtae fish as a fixed carrier.

Only the practical hanging-product grammar is retained:

- short suspension cord;
- light, wall-safe construction;
- optional compact chime;
- soft or felt backing;
- a profile that can hang flat against a door.

Personal identity remains strongest through the guardian silhouette. Elemental
palette and energy mode appear as secondary material fields and body topology.

## Recommendation

Use sample 1 as the brand image and sample 3 as the first physical prototype.

Sample 3 can be manufactured without a custom metal mold:

1. laser-cut or die-cut fabric and felt layers;
2. heat-bond or stitch the low-poly panels;
3. apply printed, woven, or embroidered elemental color fields;
4. attach a short cord and optional silent/chime hardware;
5. add a full felt back to prevent door damage.

## Personalization logic

- Guardian animal controls the outer silhouette.
- Energy mode controls pose:
  - `PURIFY`: settled or releasing;
  - `CIRCULATE`: curved continuous route;
  - `AMPLIFY`: rising or forward route.
- Target element controls one dominant and up to three supporting color fields.
- A single small metal bridge marks the prescription's main energy junction.
- Neutral material edition can remain a customer choice: leather, mineral, or
  technical textile.

## Suggested dimensions

- Height: 150–180 mm
- Maximum thickness:
  - leather: 8–12 mm;
  - mineral relief: 12–15 mm;
  - textile: 15–25 mm compressed;
- Cord drop: 40–70 mm
- Target weight:
  - soft versions: below 120 g;
  - mineral relief: below 250 g

These are image concepts, not production drawings.
