# Guardian Product Family — 3 × 3 Samples

## Shared source

- Remedy element: Earth
- Guardian: Dragon
- Energy mode: Circulate
- Source artwork:
  `docs/samples/remedy-art/guardian-style-matrix-4x3/11-totem-dragon-earth-circulate.png`
- Selected object language:
  `docs/samples/remedy-object/dragon-linked-art-object-set-5/02-ink-clay-signal.png`

The products share only a recognizable family feeling: one guardian cue, one
circulation curve, and a restrained personalized elemental palette. They are
not required to reproduce the framed artwork exactly.

## Gate Guardian — 3 modern myeongtae samples

| Sample | Positioning | Suggested production |
|---|---|---|
| `gate-01-leather-myeongtae.png` | Fashion accessory / premium gift | 160–180 mm folded vegetable-tanned leather |
| `gate-02-mineral-myeongtae.png` | Interior object / housewarming gift | 150–170 mm light wood or foamed mineral composite |
| `gate-03-seoul-tech-myeongtae.png` | Light, shippable, global street edition | 170–190 mm bonded technical textile, light padding |

The myeongtae fish is a fixed Korean gate-protection archetype. Personalization
is applied through:

- elemental color patches;
- an S-shaped circulation seam;
- a small guardian-animal emboss or embroidery;
- optional compact chime material and finish.

The customer's guardian animal should not replace the fish silhouette. This
keeps the product story culturally legible while preserving personalization.

**Recommendation:** Gate 01 is the strongest premium concept. Gate 03 is the
best candidate for inexpensive international shipping and door safety.

## Product hierarchy

1. **Pocket Guardian** — accessible entry product and checkout add-on
2. **Gate Guardian** — culturally distinctive housewarming / protection gift
3. **Desk Guardian** — personalized premium collectible
4. **Framed Remedy Artwork** — the full visual narrative behind the collection

## Production cautions

- Concept images are not production drawings.
- Desk objects require watertight CAD, balance and wall-thickness checks.
- Gate objects must be light, quiet enough for apartments, and unable to scratch
  the door; use a short suspension cord.
- Keyrings require rounded edges, minimum metal bridges, abrasion testing and
  hardware pull testing.
- Bells or chimes should be optional because some customers and apartment
  environments may prefer a silent product.
